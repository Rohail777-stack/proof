# Proof

### Preserve what people already said about you.

Proof is an open-source experiment in preserving authentic professional messages.

Your résumé tells people what you did.

**Proof preserves what people said it was like to work with you.**

---

## Why Proof?

Some of the most meaningful things people say about us at work aren't written as recommendations.

They're written in Slack messages, WhatsApp conversations, emails, farewell messages, and team chats.

They're spontaneous. Specific. Human.

And then they disappear.

People change companies. Workspaces get archived. Conversations get buried.

**Proof explores what happens if we preserve them.**

---

## How it works

Someone writes something meaningful about you.

You preserve it.

The original author receives a private verification link.

They confirm that they wrote it.

It becomes part of your Proof Archive.

```text
Someone writes something meaningful
              ↓
       Preserve it
              ↓
     Author verifies it
              ↓
       Verified Proof
              ↓
      Your Proof Archive
